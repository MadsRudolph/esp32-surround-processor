dBFS
S (green) is what goes to the rear speakers. On stereo sound it is the matrix signal, 0.5 × (L − R) after its filters and delay. On Dolby Digital it is the louder of the two real rear channels, and L and R are the decoded fronts. Last two minutes. White marks on the bars hold the highest peak of the last 10 s. 0 dBFS is digital full scale.
