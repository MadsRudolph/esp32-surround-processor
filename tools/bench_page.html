Test procedure

Work down the steps in order. Typed readings and ticks save as you go; reload keeps them. Instrument steps (D, E, F) measure on the AD3 and judge themselves; the button and tone steps (H) use the live scope and your own ticks.

Live scope two probes on anything: CH1 (orange, 1+) · CH2 (blue, 2+). Set the labels below.

edge trigger on the AD3: a repeating signal (1 kHz tone) stands still
channelmean Vmin Vmax Vp-p Vrms VAC rms Vfreqlogic

Rail watch brown-out hunt: min and max of every 80 ms, continuous at 100 kS/s

raillowest Vathighest Vdipsdip threshold
While the watch runs it owns the scope: the live view, rail check and ripple wait until you stop it. The UART console keeps running.
