0How to measure

Outside jaws for diameters and thicknesses. Close them gently on the part, then read.

Depth rod for heights above the board: rod on the board, caliper body resting on the part top. Also for depth behind a panel.

Measure in mm. One decimal is enough. Pull the jaws away before you read if the display is hard to see, or lock it with the screw.

AThe carrier board

Side view. Every arrow starts on the board top face (the face the parts stand on) and ends on the top of the part, except 1 and 7.

1

Standoff length under the board

needed before printing

Pick one of the four black standoffs. Measure its length from the underside of the board to its bottom end, with the outside jaws. Look into the bottom end for a thread. Badge 1 on drawing A.

2

Tallest part above the board

needed before printing

Rest the end of the depth rod on the board top, then slide the caliper body down until it touches the top of the tallest part. Read the value, and name the part. Badge 2 on drawing A.

3

Top of the DAC modules

needed before printing

Depth rod on the board top, caliper body resting on the highest point of a plugged-in DAC module (pins and solder included). Badge 3 on drawing A.

4

Top of the USER button

has a safe assumed value

Same method, on the top of the USER button plunger, not pressed. Badge 4 on drawing A.

5

Tops of the two LEDs

has a safe assumed value

Same method on the top of each LED dome. If the two differ, type the taller one. Badge 5 on drawing A.

6

Screw terminal and blue capacitor

has a safe assumed value

Same method twice: once on the top of a screw terminal block, once on the top of the blue 470 uF can. Badges 6a and 6b on drawing A.

7

Board thickness

has a safe assumed value

Close the outside jaws on the bare edge of the board, away from solder blobs. Badge 7 on drawing A.

BThe DIR9001 module

The green receiver board stacked above the carrier. Values 9, 10 and 14 are already settled.

8

Standoff under the DIR9001 module

needed before printing

Measure straight up from the carrier top face to the underside of the module board (the standoff length is the same number). Solder joints under the module do not count. Badge 8 on drawing B.

15

Tallest part on the module

needed before printing

Depth rod on the module top face, caliper body on the highest part (look at the can, the optical socket and the toggle). Measure from the module top face, not from the carrier. Badge 15 on drawing B.

11

Optical (TOSLINK) socket position

has a safe assumed value

11a: from the nearer short end of the module board to the middle of the socket opening (measure to the near side of the socket and add half its width). 11b: from the board edge to the front face of the socket. Badges 11a and 11b on drawing C.

12

Toggle switch reach

has a safe assumed value

12a: from the board edge to the end of the threaded bushing. 12b: from the board edge to the lever tip, lever straight out. Badges 12a and 12b on drawing C.

13

Coax RCA barrel

has a safe assumed value

From the board edge to the end of the metal barrel. Badge 13 on drawing C.

16

Pigtail length

has a safe assumed value

Lay the cable straight and measure from the end of one plug to the end of the other. A ruler is fine. The layout needs 100 or more. Badge 16 on the small drawing.

CPanel parts

The same three measurements for each jack. Part numbers are fine if a jack is not at hand. Swipe the drawing sideways if it is cut off on a phone.

A thread diameter, the hole the panel needs. B widest diameter of the nut or flange. C depth of the body and lugs behind the panel.
