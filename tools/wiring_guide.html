Speaker wiring guide

← back to the bench

One step at a time. Board power off while you wire. Nothing here can damage the board if a wire lands on the wrong screw; the worst case is silence or hum.

0The whole picture

Four outputs on the west edge of the board. Two go to the Saga, two go straight to the active speakers.

The board cannot drive a passive speaker by itself. Its outputs are line level, like a CD player's.

1Front left and right: the gold RCA posts

Two short wires per post. Do the left one, then the right one the same way.

RCA postBoard screw
centre tag (the middle hole)FL T / FR T
ground tag (the outer barrel)FL S / FR S

Then a normal RCA cable from each post to one input pair on the Saga: FL to left, FR to right.

Not sure which tag is which? DMM on continuity: the centre tag beeps to the inside of the hole, the ground tag beeps to the outer barrel.

2Surround left and right: the long cable, board end

Strip the long cable and put its three conductors into the three screws. Same for both sides.

3Surround: the plug at the speaker end

A TRS plug has three metal parts. Pick whichever option matches what you have on the desk.

Option A: a bare TRS plug soldered on (best)

White to the tip tag, brown to the ring tag, shield to the sleeve tag. Done; nothing else to think about.

Option B: reuse the plug from the short TRS-to-RCA cable

Cut the short cable, keep the TRS end, and solder the long cable onto it.

Before cutting, meter the short cable: does the plug's ring beep to anything at the RCA end?

Ring beeps to…What to do
nothing, or the RCA shellThe usual case: one core + shield inside. Join white to the core and shield to shield. Leave brown unconnected and leave screw R empty.
its own separate wireThree conductors inside: join white to the tip wire, brown to the ring wire, shield to shield, and use all three screws.

Insulate each joint (heat-shrink or tape) so the core cannot touch the shield.

With brown left out the sound is the same; you only lose the hum cancelling. If you hear hum on the long run, fit real TRS plugs later. The cable is already the right kind.

4First power-on

Tick them off in order. The ticks are remembered on this computer.

The Saga's knob only changes the fronts. The surrounds bypass it, so set their level once with the gain knob on each active speaker. One knob for all four comes later with the 10k pot on J7.
